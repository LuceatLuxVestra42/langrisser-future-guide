# External Reference Sources

These sources are provided for research, comparison, verification, terminology matching, and data-structure analysis. They are reference sources; their inclusion here does not authorize modification, deletion, bulk redistribution, or unrestricted copying.

## Chinese Bilibili Wiki

- Wiki: https://wiki.biligame.com/langrisser/首页
- Primary use: current Chinese-server information, newly released characters/content, Chinese terminology, and cross-checking structured game information.

Because wiki content can be community-maintained, verify uncertain or high-impact mappings against additional evidence when practical.

## Source-selection guidance

- Latest Chinese-server information: prefer the Bilibili Wiki and, when available, direct evidence from the current Chinese game client.
- Established Korean names and translations: use the Korean legacy references and existing Korean project data.
- Asset filenames / internal English identifiers: use the legacy asset Drive and direct client assets when available.
- Existing website implementation ideas: inspect the Korean legacy site, but do not copy its structure blindly; adapt useful patterns to the current project's UX and technical architecture.

## Conflict and uncertainty rules

- Do not invent missing values.
- Do not infer a character/skill/equipment/asset mapping solely because names look similar.
- When two sources disagree, preserve the disagreement and report it to the user unless there is strong evidence that resolves it.
- Clearly distinguish verified relationships from provisional mappings.
- External reference URLs may be consulted as needed; do not assume their contents are permanently cached or unchanged.
